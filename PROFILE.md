## About

I build **modern web and mobile products** end to end — from Figma concepts to production apps.

My focus is clean, maintainable TypeScript across the stack: **Next.js** on the frontend, **NestJS** / Node on the backend, and **PostgreSQL** (plus Mongo when it fits). I care about UI polish as much as solid architecture.

Open to collaborations, freelance work, and interesting product ideas.
